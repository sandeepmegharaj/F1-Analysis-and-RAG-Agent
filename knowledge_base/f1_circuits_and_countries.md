# Formula 1 Host Countries, Circuits and Global Calendar

## Host Countries in Modern Formula 1 (2024 Season)
The modern Formula 1 World Championship features 24 Grand Prix events across 21 countries on 5 continents:

1. **Bahrain**: Bahrain International Circuit, Sakhir
2. **Saudi Arabia**: Jeddah Corniche Circuit, Jeddah
3. **Australia**: Albert Park Circuit, Melbourne
4. **Japan**: Suzuka International Racing Course, Suzuka
5. **China**: Shanghai International Circuit, Shanghai
6. **United States**: Hosted across 3 circuits:
   - Miami International Autodrome (Florida)
   - Circuit of the Americas (COTA, Austin, Texas)
   - Las Vegas Strip Circuit (Nevada)
7. **Italy**: Hosted across 2 historic circuits:
   - Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari (Imola, Emilia Romagna)
   - Autodromo Nazionale Monza (Monza, Italian Grand Prix)
8. **Monaco**: Circuit de Monaco, Monte Carlo
9. **Canada**: Circuit Gilles Villeneuve, Montreal
10. **Spain**: Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, Montmeló
11. **Austria**: Red Bull Ring, Spielberg
12. **United Kingdom**: Silverstone Circuit, Northamptonshire
13. **Hungary**: Hungaroring, Mogyoród
14. **Belgium**: Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps, Stavelot
15. **Netherlands**: Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort
16. **Azerbaijan**: Baku City Circuit, Baku
17. **Singapore**: Marina Bay Street Circuit, Singapore
18. **Mexico**: Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez, Mexico City
19. **Brazil**: Autódromo José Carlos Pace (Interlagos), São Paulo
20. **Qatar**: Lusail International Circuit, Lusail
21. **United Arab Emirates**: Yas Marina Circuit, Abu Dhabi

## Historic Host Countries
Over 34 distinct countries have hosted official Formula 1 World Championship races since the inaugural 1950 season, including Argentina, South Africa, Portugal, France, Germany, Malaysia, India, South Korea, Turkey, and Sweden.

## Classic vs. Street Circuits
- **Permanent Classic Circuits**: Silverstone, Spa-Francorchamps, Monza, Suzuka, Red Bull Ring, Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya.
- **Street & Hybrid Circuits**: Monaco, Marina Bay (Singapore), Baku City Circuit, Jeddah Corniche, Las Vegas Strip, Miami.
