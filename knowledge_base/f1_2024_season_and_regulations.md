# Formula 1 2024 Season Teams, Drivers & Regulations

The 2024 FIA Formula 1 World Championship features 10 teams and 20 full-time drivers:

1. **Red Bull Racing**: Max Verstappen (#1) and Sergio Perez (#11) — Honda RBPT powertrain
2. **Scuderia Ferrari**: Charles Leclerc (#16) and Carlos Sainz (#55) — Ferrari powertrain
3. **Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS**: Lewis Hamilton (#44) and George Russell (#63) — Mercedes powertrain
4. **McLaren F1 Team**: Lando Norris (#4) and Oscar Piastri (#81) — Mercedes powertrain
5. **Aston Martin Aramco**: Fernando Alonso (#14) and Lance Stroll (#18) — Mercedes powertrain
6. **Alpine F1 Team**: Esteban Ocon (#31) and Pierre Gasly (#10) — Renault powertrain
7. **Williams Racing**: Alexander Albon (#23), Logan Sargeant / Franco Colapinto (#43) — Mercedes powertrain
8. **Visa Cash App RB (VCARB)**: Yuki Tsunoda (#22) and Daniel Ricciardo / Liam Lawson (#30) — Honda RBPT powertrain
9. **Kick Sauber**: Valtteri Bottas (#77) and Zhou Guanyu (#24) — Ferrari powertrain
10. **MoneyGram Haas F1 Team**: Nico Hülkenberg (#27) and Kevin Magnussen (#20) — Ferrari powertrain

## Points and Scoring Regulations
### Grand Prix Points (Top 10 Finishers)
- 1st Place (Winner): 25 points
- 2nd Place: 18 points
- 3rd Place: 15 points
- 4th Place: 12 points
- 5th Place: 10 points
- 6th Place: 8 points
- 7th Place: 6 points
- 8th Place: 4 points
- 9th Place: 2 points
- 10th Place: 1 point

### Fastest Lap Bonus
- 1 bonus point is awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the Grand Prix, provided they finish inside the top 10 positions.

### Sprint Race Points (Top 8 Finishers)
Sprint races award points to the top 8 finishers:
- 1st: 8 pts, 2nd: 7 pts, 3rd: 6 pts, 4th: 5 pts, 5th: 4 pts, 6th: 3 pts, 7th: 2 pts, 8th: 1 pt.
