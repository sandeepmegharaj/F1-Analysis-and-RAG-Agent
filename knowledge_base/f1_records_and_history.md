# Formula 1 All-Time Records, Statistics & Champions

## Highest Scoring Drivers in F1 History
- **Lewis Hamilton**: All-time leader with over 4,800+ career points. Hamilton also holds the record for the most Grand Prix wins (105+) and pole positions (104+).
- **Sebastian Vettel**: Over 3,098 career points, 4 World Championships (2010–2013).
- **Max Verstappen**: Over 3,000+ career points, 3+ World Championships (2021–2023+), record for most wins in a single season (19 wins in 2023).
- **Fernando Alonso**: Over 2,300 career points, 2 World Championships (2005, 2006), most race starts in F1 history (over 400 Grand Prix entries).
- **Michael Schumacher**: 1,566 career points (scored predominantly under the older 10-6-4-3-2-1 and 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 systems; adjusted to modern points would be well over 4,000), 7 World Championships, 91 wins.

*Note on Scoring Systems*: Prior to 2010, race winners received 10 points (and earlier 9 or 8). The modern 25-point system introduced in 2010 significantly increased points totals for modern drivers compared to historical legends.

## Most World Drivers' Championships
- **Michael Schumacher**: 7 (1994, 1995, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004)
- **Lewis Hamilton**: 7 (2008, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020)
- **Juan Manuel Fangio**: 5 (1951, 1954, 1955, 1956, 1957)
- **Alain Prost**: 4 (1985, 1986, 1989, 1993)
- **Sebastian Vettel**: 4 (2010, 2011, 2012, 2013)
- **Max Verstappen**: 3 (2021, 2022, 2023)
- **Ayrton Senna**: 3 (1988, 1990, 1991)
- **Niki Lauda, Jackie Stewart, Jack Brabham, Nelson Piquet**: 3 each

## Most Constructors' Championships
- **Scuderia Ferrari**: 16 Constructors' Championships (most in F1 history)
- **Williams Racing**: 9 Championships
- **McLaren**: 8 Championships
- **Mercedes-AMG Petronas**: 8 consecutive Championships (2014–2021)
- **Red Bull Racing**: 6 Championships (2010–2013, 2022–2023)
